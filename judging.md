JUDGING CRITERIA
Online Phase Judging Criteria
These are the criteria to be evaluated in this round, based on the PDF Proposal Idea submitted by each team on the DevPost platform:
Problem Relevance & Solution Fit (25%) - Online Phase
Assess the extent to which the issues raised are real, urgent, and supported by valid data. Also, assess whether the solutions proposed in the proposal truly address these issues in a logical and targeted manner.
Technical Architecture & Feasibility (20%) - Online Phase
Evaluate the logical system architecture and the suitability of the chosen tech stack. Also, assess the feasibility of the system design for implementation as a Minimum Viable Product (MVP).
Innovation & Value Proposition (30%) - Online Phase
Determine the level of originality of the idea and the extent to which the proposed solution is novel, innovative, and possesses a unique selling point that distinguishes it from similar applications or solutions already on the market.
Market & Impact Viability (15%) - Online Phase
Determine the potential for the solution to have a tangible impact when implemented in a real-world ecosystem, the clarity of the target market (users), the sustainability, and the scalability of the application.
Document Clarity & Structure (10%) - Online Phase
Assessing the clarity, completeness, and neatness of the document. This includes the ease of following the line of reasoning, a professional writing structure, and adherence to specified formatting guidelines.
Offline Phase Judging Criteria
These judging criteria will transition from the DevPost platform to the compsphere.id website; only teams that advance past the online round will be able to access the team dashboard and submit their work on the compsphere.id platform:
Functional Project & Live Demo (35%) - Offline Phase
Evaluating the technical validation and stability of the product. The judges will ensure that key features (Web3, AI, Blockchain, etc.) actually function. Projects that have been deployed live are preferred over those running on localhost.
Problem-Solution Fit & Public Impact (30%) - Offline Phase
Validating whether the built MVP is practical and relevant in solving real, data-driven problems. The evaluation focuses on applicability within the industry sector and potential for future scalability.
Creative Tech-Implementation (20%) - Offline Phase
Testing the efficiency and creativity of the architecture in integrating the tech stack (Web3, AI, IoT, Web2) within 24 hours. The assessment covers the reliability of the system logic and an audit of the commit history (GitHub/GitLab).
Pitching & Q&A Defense (15%) - Offline Phase
Evaluate the team’s communication skills and cohesion when presenting the product and defending technical/business arguments before the judges. This evaluation covers the entire process, from initial preparation through the Q&A session.
